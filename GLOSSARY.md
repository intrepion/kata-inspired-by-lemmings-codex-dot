# Mosslight Rescue
A garden rescue campaign in which glowling crews travel to lantern shelters.

## Language
**Glowling**: A small autonomous traveler entrusted to the player. _Avoid_: Lemming, unit.

**Shelter**: The destination where a glowling is rescued. _Avoid_: goal, door.

**Quota**: The minimum number of glowling rescues needed to complete a garden.

**Charge**: One available use of an assignable skill.

**Garden**: One puzzle with its own terrain, crew, shelter and charges. _Avoid_: map.

**Run**: A single attempt at a garden, ending with success or failure.

**Bramble**: A fatal region of a garden.

**Rootstone**: Terrain that excavation cannot remove.
